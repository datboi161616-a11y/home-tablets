Calendars

Show your phone’s calendar on the tablet. Paste its private link:

iPhone: where’s the link?
  1. Open the Calendar app and tap Calendars at the bottom.
  2. Tap the ⓘ next to the calendar you want.
  3. Turn on Public Calendar, then tap Share Link… → Copy.
  4. Paste it here. (It starts with webcal://.)
Google Calendar: where’s the link?
  1. On a computer, open calendar.google.com → ⚙ Settings.
  2. On the left, click your calendar’s name.
  3. Scroll to Secret address in iCal format and copy it.
  4. Paste it here. (It ends in basic.ics.)

The tablet reads the calendar; it can’t change it. Add events on your phone as usual.
