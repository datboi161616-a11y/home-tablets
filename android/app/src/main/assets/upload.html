Calendars

Show your phone’s calendar on the kitchen screen. Paste its private link:

iPhone: where’s the link?
  1. Open the Calendar app and tap Calendars at the bottom.
  2. Tap the ⓘ next to the calendar you want.
  3. Scroll down, turn on Public Calendar, then tap Share Link… → Copy.
  4. Come back here, tap + Add a calendar link, then press and hold in the link box and tap Paste. (It starts with webcal://.)
Google Calendar: where’s the link?
  1. This part needs a computer: the Google Calendar phone app doesn’t show the link.
  2. On the computer, open calendar.google.com and click ⚙ → Settings.
  3. On the left, under Settings for my calendars, click your calendar’s name.
  4. Scroll right down to Secret address in iCal format, click the copy button next to it.
  5. Send it to yourself (email or message), open it on this phone, copy it and paste it here. (It ends in basic.ics.)
Outlook / Hotmail: where’s the link?
  1. On a computer, open outlook.com → ⚙ Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars.
  2. Under Publish a calendar, choose the calendar and Can view all details, then Publish.
  3. Copy the ICS link (not the HTML one) and paste it here.

The kitchen screen reads the calendar; it can’t change it. Add events on your phone as usual. Keep the link to yourself: anyone who has it can see the calendar.
