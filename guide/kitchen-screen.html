Your kitchen screen

It stays on, looks after itself, and gets new things by itself. Here is everything worth knowing, on one page.

The two screens you’ll use most

Home shows the clock, the weather, your photos, tonight’s dinner and what’s coming up. Tap any of them for more.

Menu (the button beside the greeting) has everything else as big tiles.

Photos from any phone

On the screen: Photos → Add photos. Point your phone’s camera at the code and pick pictures. No app, no account; the phone just has to be on your home Wi-Fi.

That same page on your phone can also leave a Note, show the shopping List at the store, send a family Recipe, and (under Settings) add birthdays and your Google or iPhone calendar.

All photos on the photo frame shows every picture, and takes off the ones you choose.

Timers

Timers on Home: tap a time to start, tap a name (Pasta, Oven…) if you like. They keep counting on every screen, and ring until someone taps Stop.

Cooking

Recipes: search by dish or by what’s in the fridge. On a recipe, Cook now gives big steps you can read from across the kitchen, with a timer on the steps that need one.

Week plan: tap + Add under a day. The Shopping list writes itself from the plan; add your own things with one tap.

Calendar and notes

Calendar → + Add puts something on with a few taps. Holidays and birthdays are there already.

Messages → + New note leaves a note for whoever walks by next.

At night

From 10 PM, once nobody has touched it for a few minutes, it dims to a quiet clock, and wakes with you in the morning. Tap it any time to use it.

Make it yours

Menu → Settings: your family’s name, 12 or 24-hour clock, your town for the weather, how bright and how loud, the alarm sound, when the night clock comes on.

Wipe the screen (on the Menu) switches touches off for 30 seconds while you clean the glass.

If something isn’t right

A word in the corner tells you when the charger has come out or the Wi-Fi is gone. Keep it plugged in.

Looks stuck? Menu → Settings → Wi-Fi & about → Start again. Nothing is lost.

New Wi-Fi or a new router? Settings → Wi-Fi & about → Wi-Fi settings, and enter the grown-up PIN.
