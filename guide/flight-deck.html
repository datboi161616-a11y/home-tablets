CAPTAIN TOM · FLIGHT DECK

Real planes, real rockets, the real night sky, for where you live. It stays on, and it looks after itself. Here is the whole deck on one page.

The deck

The main screen shows the planes over your house right now, how far Voyager 1 has gone, tonight’s Moon, and when the Space Station next flies over. The big buttons along the bottom open everything else. ◂ DECK at the top left of any screen brings you back.

Planes overhead

A radar of every aircraft within 25 miles. Tap one: where it came from, where it’s going, what it is, and where to look out of the window. RANGE switches to 10 miles when the sky is busy.

Departures

An airport board for the busiest airport near you. Tap a flight to see it on the radar; the buttons at the top switch between nearby airports.

Tonight’s sky

The one best thing to look at tonight, which planets are up and how high, the Moon, and every visible pass of the Space Station (tap its row). TOMORROW NIGHT peeks ahead.

Space missions

The next rocket launch, counting down, and what is flying now. Tap the launch for every launch coming up, in plain words.

Sky alert

The deck turns red by itself when something is worth going outside for: the Space Station passing, a launch about to go, a rare plane. Tap SEEN IT to put it away.

Trivia and pictures

TRIVIA: a question of the day and rounds of five; earn your wings. DEEP IMAGE (top of the deck): NASA’s picture of the day, the past week’s, and the story behind each.

At night

From 10 PM, once it has been left alone a few minutes, the deck dims to a quiet starfield. Tap anywhere to come back.

Set it up your way

SETTINGS (top of the deck): your town (for the sky, the planes and the weather), 12 or 24-hour clock, brightness, sound, when the starfield takes over, and Wipe the screen for cleaning the glass.

If something isn’t right

A word in the corner says when the charger has come out or the Wi-Fi is gone. Keep it plugged in. If it looks stuck: SETTINGS → Wi-Fi & about → Start again. A panel marked SAMPLE is only an example while the live feed is away; it comes back by itself.
